Authentic Old-World Method

The 4-Step Kettle-to-Hearth Alchemy

Real bagels cannot be faked with steam injectors or chemical dough conditioners. Here is our 100-year-old time-honored baking process and fresh raw ingredients.

01

36h Cold Fermentation

High-protein wheat dough kneaded with wild levain, then cold-rested for 36 hours in cellars to develop tangy depth and chewy airy crumb.

02

Malt Kettle Poaching

Hand-rolled bagels submerge in a raging 212°F bath infused with dark barley malt syrup for 90 seconds, setting the shiny blistered shell.

03

Cedar Board & Hearth

Deck-baked first on water-soaked burlap cedar boards, then flipped directly onto 480°F fiery brick stone to sear the golden blister.

04

Small-Batch Schmears

Local cultured cream cheese whipped by hand every morning with garden scallions, fresh roasted garlic, wild berries, and blossom honey.

Live Stone Hearth • 480°F Brick Deck

Our master bakers operate the historic fire oven from 4:00 AM daily, flipping bagels by hand with six-foot cedar peels.
