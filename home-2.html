Culinary Chemistry

The 4-Pillar Craft Alchemy

A bagel is not merely round bread with a hole. True artisan bagelcraft requires an exact interplay of high gluten hydration, malt starch gelatinization, and heat shock.

Micro-Blister Texture

The Thousand-Blister Crust

Proof of cold fermentation and barley-malt kettle poaching.

01

14.2% High-Gluten Flour

We mill unbleached Montana hard red spring wheat. The high protein matrix creates the dense, toothsome resistance unique to real kettle bagels.

02

Barley Malt Poaching

Boiling for 90 seconds in rich malt extract gelatinizes surface starches, sealing interior moisture and guaranteeing a deep glossy amber sheen.

03

Cedar Plank Steaming

Bagels enter the hearth on wet cedar boards wrapped in burlap. Steam locks in the rise before they are flipped directly onto volcanic stone.

04

Volcanic Stone Shock

Direct stone contact at 520°F creates instant caramelization, blistered bubbles, and that inimitable hollow crunch when bitten into.
