Our Bakery Story —
How We Started on Orchard Street.

In the autumn of 1928, master baker Solomon Katz arrived in Lower Manhattan carrying a single wicker basket holding an active wild sourdough levain crock. In a narrow brick basement beneath Orchard Street, he built a roaring wood-fired stone oven and set out to bake bagels the centuries-old way: hand-shaped, fermented for 36 hours in cold cellars, and kettle-poached in pure barley malt.

While industrial bread factories in the 1960s switched to steam injection, chemical dough relaxers, and conveyor belts, our family made an oath: we would never abandon the stone hearth, never rush the dough, and never bake a bagel that hadn't bathed in rolling kettle water.

The Immutable Laws of The Hearth

Our Baking Philosophy & Bagel-Making Tradition

A true New York bagel is not a roll with a hole in the middle. It is a specific, ancient thermodynamic transformation governed by these four sacred baking tenets.

The 90-Second Malt Baptism

Gelatinizing the surface starches in pure dark barley malt creates the golden, blistered chew.

Living Mountain Water

We filter regional mountain water to achieve the precise natural calcium and magnesium balance required for optimal gluten strength and crust tension.

36-Hour Cold Dormancy

Never fast-proofed in hot boxes. Our dough rests for 36 hours at 38°F, allowing wild enzymes to slowly develop deep lactic acidity and digestible crumb.

Malt Kettle Poaching

No steam tunnels. Bagels submerge directly in rolling 212°F kettles steeped with unrefined barley malt syrup, locking in the dense, chewy center.

Wet Cedar to Stone Fire

Seeded bagels steam upside-down on wet burlap cedar planks before being turned onto the bare 480°F brick deck for a blistering oven finish.

Zero Additives • 100% Transparency

Ingredient Selection & Freshness Standards

We believe what you leave out is as important as what you put in. Our core bagel dough contains exactly five pure, non-GMO pantry ingredients.

Hudson Valley Unbleached Wheat

14.2% Protein

Milled within 120 miles from upstate New York winter wheat. Unbromated, unbleached, and rich in natural wheat germ and nutty aromatics.

Non-GMO Barley Malt Syrup

Heritage Malthouse

Slow-sprouted barley providing natural complex sugars for levain fermentation, giving the crust its deep amber luster without refined cane sugar.

Pure Atlantic Flake Sea Salt

Mineral Rich

Harvested from pristine ocean currents, providing clean salinity that accentuates grain sweetness without bitter chemical anticaking agents.

The 4-Hour Freshness Law

Zero Stale Rule

Every bagel in our baskets was boiled and stone-baked within the last 4 hours. At 4:00 PM closing, any leftover bagels are packed fresh and donated directly to local shelters.
