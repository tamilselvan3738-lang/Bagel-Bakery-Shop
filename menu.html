The Sweet Boil Technique

Unlike mass-produced pastries with sugar coatings, our sweet bagels maintain the authentic chewy NY kettle-boiled bagel texture. We blend raw wildflower honey directly into the boiling kettle, creating a glossy, crackling caramelization on the outer shell while keeping the inside tender and moist.
